# Appendix: The Cheat Sheet

This is where the lists live. The first half collects the word families from chapters three through seven in full, for browsing and raiding. The second half is formatted for flashcards: root, meaning, and enough familiar words to hang it on.

## Latin verb families

- **FAC / FIC / FACT / FECT** (make, do; *facere*): factory, fact, facsimile, fax, manufacture, benefactor, malefactor, satisfy, efficient, deficit, sufficient, proficient, sacrifice (make sacred), magnificent, perfect, infect (originally to dye or stain), defect, effect, affect.
- **CAP / CIP / CAPT / CEPT** (take, seize; *capere*): capture, captive, capable, accept, except, concept, intercept, perceive, receive, reception, deceive, anticipate, participate, recipe, recipient, emancipate, municipal (*munus*, duty: taking on civic duties).
- **DUC / DUCT** (lead; *ducere*): duke, duct, aqueduct, viaduct, conduct, induce, deduce, reduce, produce, seduce (lead aside), introduce, abduct, ductile (of metals that can be drawn into wire), educate (from the cousin verb *educare*, to bring up).
- **MIT / MISS** (send; *mittere*): mission, missile, missionary, transmit, emit, permit, submit, admit, commit (entrust), omit, dismiss, promise, remit, intermittent (sending between: stopping and starting), compromise.
- **PORT** (carry; *portare*): portable, porter, export, import, report, support, transport, deport, portfolio (carrier of leaves), important (carrying something in with it), opportunity, sport.
- **FER / LAT** (carry, bear; *ferre*, *latum*): refer / relate, transfer / translate, confer / collate, prefer / prelate, infer / illation, legislator, superlative, fertile, circumference, suffer (bear from below).
- **SPEC / SPIC / SPECT** (look; *specere*): inspect, respect, suspect (look at from below), expect, prospect, retrospect, introspect, circumspect, spectacle, spectator, spectrum, specter, speculate, conspicuous, despise (look down on), perspective, auspicious, species.
- **VID / VIS** (see; *videre*): video, vision, visible, visit, evident, provide, providence, prudence, improvise, supervise, revise, advise, envy, invidious, interview, view.
- **DIC / DICT** (say; *dicere*): dictate, dictionary, diction, dictator, predict, contradict, verdict, benediction, malediction, edict, indict, addict, dedicate, abdicate, jurisdiction, condition (a term spoken together).
- **SCRIB / SCRIPT** (write; *scribere*): scribe, script, scripture, describe, prescribe, subscribe (sign underneath), inscribe, transcribe, manuscript, postscript, conscript, proscribe (post as an outlaw, hence forbid), ascribe, circumscribe, nondescript.
- **STA / STAT / SIST** (stand; *stare*, *sistere*): station, stable, statue, state, status, statute, static, constant, distant, instant, circumstance, substance, obstacle, insist, resist, persist, consist, assist, exist, desist, armistice, solstice, superstition, ecstasy (Greek).
- **CED / CESS** (go, yield; *cedere*): proceed, recede, precede, exceed, succeed, concede, accede, secede, intercede, access, process, excess, recess, predecessor, ancestor, cease, necessary.
- **PON / POS / POSIT** (put, place; *ponere*): position, positive (set down, settled), deposit, compose, component, opponent, propose, postpone, expose, impose, suppose, dispose, oppose, transpose, preposition, repository, composite, compost, posture, post.
- **TEN / TIN / TENT / TAIN** (hold; *tenere*): tenant, tenacious, tenure, tenet, content, continent, contain, retain, detain, sustain, maintain (hold in the hand), obtain, pertain, abstain, entertain (hold between, keep occupied), lieutenant.
- **TEND / TENS** (stretch; *tendere*; not the same root as *tenere*): tense, tension, intend, extend, attend, pretend (stretch forth a claim).
- **JAC / JECT** (throw; *jacere*): project, reject, inject, eject, object, subject, adjective (thrown toward a noun), conjecture, trajectory, abject, interjection, dejected, jettison, jet.
- **STRING / STRICT** (draw tight, bind; *stringere*): strict, stringent, constrict, restrict, district, restrain, constrain, strain, stress, distress, prestige.
- **PEL / PULS** (drive, push; *pellere*): propel, repel, expel, compel, dispel, impulse, compulsory, pulse, repulsive, propeller.
- **VEN / VENT** (come; *venire*): convene, convention, convenient, event, invent, prevent, advent, adventure, avenue, revenue, venue, intervene, circumvent, souvenir.
- **VOC / VOK** (call, voice; *vocare*): vocal, vocabulary, vocation, vociferous, advocate, provoke, revoke, evoke, invoke, convoke, equivocal (calling by equal names), irrevocable, avocation (a calling away from real work: a hobby).

## Latin noun families

- **CAPUT / CAPIT** (head): capital, capitalism, decapitate, per capita, capitulate, recapitulate, precipice, precipitate (headlong), chapter, chapiter, cabbage, chief, chef, cattle, chattel, captain, cape, corporal (via Italian *caporale*).
- **MANUS** (hand): manual, manufacture, manuscript, manicure, manipulate, manacle, manner, manage, maintain, emancipate, mandate, command, demand, recommend, legerdemain (light of hand), manure, maneuver.
- **PES / PED** (foot): pedal, pedestrian, pedestal, biped, quadruped, centipede, impede, expedite, expedition, pedigree, pawn, pioneer, pedometer.
- **COR / CORD** (heart): cordial, concord, discord, accord, record, courage, core (possibly), misericord. Greek *kardia*: cardiac, cardiology, cardiovascular.
- **CORPUS** (body): corpse, corps, corporation, corporal (bodily), corpulent, incorporate, corpuscle, habeas corpus. Greek *soma*: somatic, psychosomatic, chromosome (colored body).
- **ANIMA** (breath, soul): animal, animate, inanimate, animation, unanimous, magnanimous, pusillanimous, equanimity, animosity.
- **MENS / MENT** (mind): mental, dementia, mention, comment, reminisce, memento. Greek *psyche*: psychology, psychiatry, psychosomatic, psychedelic.
- **LUX / LUC, LUMEN** (light): lucid, elucidate, translucent, Lucifer, pellucid, luminous, illuminate, luminary.
- **CANDERE** (glow white): candle, incandescent, candid, candor, candidate.
- **The elements and the sky**: *terra* (terrain, territory, terrace, subterranean, extraterrestrial, Mediterranean, terrier); *aqua* (aquarium, aqueduct, aquatic, aquifer); *ignis* (ignite, igneous, ignition); *mare* (marine, submarine, maritime, mariner); *sol* (solar, solstice, parasol); *luna* (lunar, lunatic, lunacy); *stella* (stellar, constellation, interstellar); *sidus* (sidereal, consider, desire). Greek parallels: *geo-*, *hydro-*, *pyro-*, *thalasso-* (sea), *helio-*, *seleno-* (moon), *astro-*.

## Greek families

- **LOGOS** (word, reason, study): biology, geology, psychology, theology, anthropology, etymology, dialogue, monologue, prologue, epilogue, catalogue, analogue, logic, apology, eulogy, syllogism.
- **GRAPH / GRAM** (write, draw): graph, graphic, graphite, paragraph, autograph, biography, geography, photograph, telegraph, phonograph, seismograph, calligraphy, pornography, cryptography, choreography, telegram, diagram, program, anagram, grammar, glamour, monogram, kilogram.
- **PATH** (feeling, suffering): sympathy, empathy, antipathy, apathy, pathetic, pathology, pathogen, psychopath, sociopath, telepathy, homeopathy, osteopathy, neuropathy, naturopathy.
- **TOM** (cut): atom, anatomy, dichotomy, appendectomy, tonsillectomy, lobotomy, tracheotomy, epitome, tome, entomology. (Not *-ostomy*, from *stoma*, mouth.)
- **ERG / URG** (work): energy, ergonomic, synergy, allergy, metallurgy, dramaturgy, liturgy, surgery, George, orgy. With *cheir* (hand): chirurgeon, chiropractor.
- **KRATOS** (-cracy, power): democracy, aristocracy, plutocracy, theocracy, bureaucracy, meritocracy, kakistocracy.
- **ARCHE** (-archy, rule; also beginning): monarchy, oligarchy, anarchy, patriarchy, matriarchy, hierarchy, archaic, archaeology.
- **GEN** (birth, kind): genesis, gene, genetic, generate, genus, genre, gender, general, generous, genius, genial, genuine, indigenous, ingenious, engine, progeny, congenital, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, pathogen, carcinogen.
- **The rest**: *chron-* time (chronic, chronology, anachronism, synchronize); *phon-* sound (telephone, symphony, phonetic, cacophony); *phot-* light (photograph, photon, photosynthesis); *scop-* look (telescope, microscope, periscope, horoscope); *metr-* measure (thermometer, geometry, symmetry, diameter); *phil-* love (philosophy, philanthropy, bibliophile, Philadelphia); *phob-* fear (claustrophobia, hydrophobia, xenophobia); *morph-* form (morphology, amorphous, metamorphosis, morphine); *nom-* law, management (economy, astronomy, autonomy, taxonomy); *cosm-* order, world (cosmos, cosmology, cosmopolitan, cosmetic); *techn-* craft (technology, technique, pyrotechnics); *bio-* life (biology, antibiotic, symbiosis); *zo-* animal (zoo, zoology, protozoa, zodiac); *anthrop-* human (anthropology, philanthropy, misanthrope); *dem-* people (democracy, epidemic, demographic); *pol-* city (politics, police, metropolis, cosmopolitan); *the-* god (theology, atheist, pantheon, enthusiasm); *hydr-* water (hydraulic, dehydrate, hydrogen); *therm-* heat (thermometer, thermal, thermostat); *ge-* earth (geology, geography, geometry); *astr-* star (astronomy, astrology, disaster, asterisk, astronaut).

The body-part roots they attach to: *cardi-* (heart), *neur-* (nerve), *hepat-* (liver), *nephr-* (kidney), *gastr-* (stomach), *oste-* (bone), *arthr-* (joint), *derm-* (skin), *hem-* or *hemat-* (blood), *cephal-* (head), *encephal-* (brain: in the head), *ophthalm-* (eye), *ot-* (ear), *rhin-* (nose), *pneum-* (lung, breath), *my-* (muscle), *cyt-* (cell), *hist-* (tissue). Once you have this list, *hepatitis, nephrology, gastroenterologist, encephalopathy, osteosarcoma,* and *rhinoplasty* are all things you can read without ever having seen them.
